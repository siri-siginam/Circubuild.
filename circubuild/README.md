# Circubuild – Construction & Demolition Waste Reuse Platform

## About the Project
Circubuild is a platform that promotes the reuse and recycling of construction and demolition waste by connecting reusable materials with people who need them. It aims to reduce waste, lower costs, and encourage sustainable construction practices.

## Objectives
- Promote the reuse of construction materials.
- Reduce construction and demolition waste.
- Minimize environmental pollution.
- Encourage sustainable construction.

## Key Features
- Platform for reusable construction materials.
- Helps connect material providers with potential users.
- Promotes eco-friendly and cost-effective construction.
